METRIC DEFINITIONS

How to interpret

Waiting time

Time between job submission and the beginning of printing.

Turnaround time

Time between job submission and completion.

Printer utilization

Busy printers divided by all configured printers at this instant. It is a snapshot, not historical utilization.

Experiment tip

Submit a similar set of jobs under FCFS, SJF, and Priority + Aging, then compare completed-job waiting time. Resetting the demo database between controlled experiments helps keep results comparable.
